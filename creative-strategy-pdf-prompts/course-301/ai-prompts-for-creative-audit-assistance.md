How to Use These Prompts
These prompts will help you analyze the data you extract, identify patterns, and generate
insights. Use these prompts after you've pulled the relevant data from your ad platforms.

I've created a set of AI prompts designed specifically to help creative strategists analyze
their ad data more efficiently. These prompts fall into three categories:


Data Analysis Assistance

   These prompts help identify patterns in top performers

   Analyze hook and hold rates

   Assess creative fatigue

   Compare format performance

   Understand audience response differences


Strategic Interpretation

   Generate creative test plans based on audit findings

   Develop comprehensive creative briefs

   Create 90-day performance improvement roadmaps

   Analyze competitive creative approaches

   Calculate the impact of specific creative elements



Data Visualization Requests

   Design effective visualizations of creative performance

   Create decision trees for creative development


Each prompt is structured to work with data you've already extracted from your ad
platforms. You simply paste your data into the prompt template and the AI will help you
identify patterns, generate insights, and develop strategic recommendations.

Data Analysis Assistance Prompts
1. Pattern Identification in Top Performers

  I've pulled data on my top-performing ads from the last 30 days. Help me
  identify patterns and commonalities in these creatives:

  [Paste a table or list of your top performers with:
  - Brief creative description
  - Key metrics (ROAS, CPA, CTR, etc.)
  - Format (video/image/carousel)
  - Key message points
  - Target audience]

  Please analyze this data and identify:
  1. Common visual elements among top performers
  2. Messaging patterns or themes that appear repeatedly
  3. Format preferences (video length, image style, etc.)
  4. Any outliers worth noting
  5. Initial hypotheses about what's driving performance




2. Hook & Hold Rate Analysis

  I need help analyzing hook and hold rates for my video creatives. Here's
  my data:

  [Paste a table with:
  - Creative name/ID
  - Hook rate (3s views/impressions)
  - Hold rate (ThruPlays/impressions)
  - Conversion rate
  - Brief description of video opening]

  Based on this data, please:
  1. Identify patterns in high-performing hooks
  2. Analyze the relationship between hook rate and conversion
  3. Identify patterns in videos with strong hold rates
  4. Suggest 3-5 specific hooks to test in upcoming creatives
  5. Recommend elements to incorporate for better viewer retention
