AI Prompts for Review Analysis

Prompt 1

Extract Key Insights from Reviews

  I need help analyzing customer reviews for [PRODUCT CATEGORY]. Here are
  [NUMBER] reviews from [SOURCE]:

  [PASTE 10-20 REVIEWS]

  Please analyze these reviews and provide:
  1. The top 5 most frequently mentioned benefits/positive aspects
  2. The top 5 most common pain points/negative aspects
  3. Emotional language patterns (specific words that indicate strong
  feelings)
  4. Recurring phrases that could be used in marketing copy
  5. Any unexpected or surprising insights
  6. The apparent customer awareness level (are they familiar with
  solutions, just recognizing the problem, etc.)




Prompt 2

Identify Patterns Across Multiple Products

  I'm researching customer preferences in the [INDUSTRY/CATEGORY] market.
  Here are reviews from 3 competing products:

  Product A: [PASTE 5-7 REVIEWS]
  Product B: [PASTE 5-7 REVIEWS]
  Product C: [PASTE 5-7 REVIEWS]

  Please identify:
  1. Common pain points across all products
  2. Features/benefits that consistently delight customers
  3. Gaps or unmet needs mentioned across multiple products
  4. The language patterns and terminology customers use when discussing
  these products
  5. How customers compare products to each other (what factors matter most)

   PART 5



Automating Your Research Workflow
Setting Up Your Research System
Here's a recommended end-to-end workflow to automate your audience research:

1.Data Collection Phase
   Use web scraping tools like Octoparse or ParseHub to collect reviews and forum posts
   Set up Mention or Brandwatch alerts for relevant keywords
   Use IFTTT to automatically save social media mentions to a spreadsheet
   Schedule regular exports from Answer the Public for keyword questions


2. Analysis Phase
      Process collected data through Claude or ChatGPT using the prompts above
      Use MonkeyLearn for sentiment analysis of large review datasets
      Import transcribed customer interviews from Otter.ai
      Create custom analysis models in Obviously AI for pattern recognition



3. Organization Phase
      Store all insights in a central repository like Notion or Airtable
      Create a database of audience language organized by persona and pain point
      Develop a swipe file of high-performing copy using Sunsama or Evernote
      Set up regular review cycles using Asana or ClickUp



4. Application Phase
      Use Claude to generate creative approaches based on research findings
      Test messaging through platforms like Wynter or Userlytics
      Track performance data with Supermetrics to identify winning approaches
      Continually feed performance data back into your research system

Important Reminder
While these AI tools and prompts can dramatically accelerate your research
process, they work best when:

 1. You understand the fundamental principles behind audience research
 2. You can critically evaluate the AI outputs against your business knowledge
 3. You're looking for patterns across large datasets, not just summary statistics
 4. You continue to validate findings with real customer interactions
 5. You recognize that AI may miss cultural nuances or emerging trends

AI is your research partner, not a replacement for developing your customer
empathy and understanding. Use these tools to scale what you've learned in this
module, not to skip learning the fundamental skills.
