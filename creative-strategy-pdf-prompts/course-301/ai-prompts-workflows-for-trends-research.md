Trend Identification Prompts
Prompt 1

Platform-Specific Trend Analysis

  I need help identifying current organic content trends on [PLATFORM]
  for the [INDUSTRY/NICHE] category.

  Based on your knowledge of trending content patterns:

  1. What are 5-7 specific content formats or approaches that are
  currently popular on [PLATFORM] in this category?

  2. For each trend, please describe:
     - The typical hook style
     - Visual characteristics
     - Structural pattern (how it typically unfolds)
     - Why you think it's resonating with audiences

  3. Which of these trends have the most potential for brand adaptation,
  and why?

  4. What specific hashtags should I search to find more examples of each
  trend?



Prompt 2

Platform-Specific Trend Analysis

   I'm researching content trends across multiple platforms for my
   [PRODUCT/SERVICE]. Help me identify patterns that are showing up across
   platforms.

   Please analyze current trends on TikTok, Instagram Reels, and YouTube
   Shorts and identify:

   1. 3-5 content structures that are working across multiple platforms
   2. How these structures are being slightly modified for each platform's
   unique environment
   3. Which underlying psychological triggers these trends are leveraging
   (curiosity, surprise, etc.)
   4. Specific examples of how brands in my industry could adapt these
   cross-platform trends

   My product/service: [BRIEF DESCRIPTION]
   My target audience: [BRIEF DESCRIPTION]

Prompt 3

Trend Lifecycle Assessment

   I've identified these potential trends I'm considering for my creative
   strategy:

   [LIST 3-5 TRENDS YOU'VE SPOTTED]

   For each trend, please help me assess:

   1. Its current position in the trend lifecycle (early, peak, declining)
   2. Estimated remaining lifespan (in weeks/months)

   4. The risks of adopting it too late
   5. The potential for creating an "evergreen" version that could last
   beyond the trend

   Based on this analysis, which trend(s) should I prioritize for
   immediate development?

Trend Analysis & Adaptation Prompts
Prompt 4

Trend Structure Breakdown

   I need to analyze this trending content format to understand its
   structure:

   Trend name/description: [DESCRIBE TREND]
   Links to examples: [PASTE 1-3 LINKS IF POSSIBLE]

   Please break down:

   1. The precise hook structure (first 3 seconds)
   2. The content flow and timing (where transitions occur, when reveals
   happen)
   3. The audio approach (music changes, sound effects, voiceover
   patterns)
   4. Text and visual elements that make it distinctive

   Then, help me create a versatile template that preserves this structure
   but could be adapted for my [PRODUCT/SERVICE] while maintaining what
   makes the trend effective.



Prompt 5

Brand Adaptation Strategist

   I want to adapt this trending format for my brand:

   Trend description: [DESCRIBE TREND]
   My brand/product: [DESCRIBE YOUR PRODUCT]
   My audience: [DESCRIBE TARGET AUDIENCE]

   Please help me:

   1. Identify which elements of the trend are essential to keep vs. which
   can be modified
   2. Create 3 different hook variations that maintain the trend's
   structure but fit my brand
   3. Suggest how to naturally incorporate my product/benefit while
   preserving the trend's feel
   4. Develop a script outline that follows the trend structure but tells
   my brand story

   The goal is to leverage the trend's popularity while ensuring the
   content feels authentic to my brand.

Research Workflows
Workflow 1

Weekly Trend Monitoring System

Step 1
Set Up Dedicated Research Accounts
     Create platform accounts that follow only trend-setting creators in your category
         Follow 20-30 creators who consistently produce high-engagement content
         Do not follow brands/competitors (focus on organic creator content)


Step 2
Use AI For Initial Scanning


   Based on my research focus on [INDUSTRY/NICHE], please suggest:

   1. 10-15 specific creators to follow who consistently set trends in
   this space
   2. 10-20 hashtags I should monitor regularly for emerging trends
   3. 5-7 sounds/audio trends to pay attention to currently
   4. A schedule for how often I should check each of these sources




Step 3
Weekly Analysis With AI
After your weekly research session, use this prompt with your findings


   I've identified these potential content patterns in my weekly research:

   [LIST 3-5 PATTERNS YOU'VE NOTICED]

   For each pattern:
   1. Is this truly trending or just a coincidence?
   2. What's driving its popularity (psychological trigger, platform
   algorithm preference, etc.)?
   3. How could it be adapted for commercial content?
   4. What brands are already leveraging this effectively?
   5. Should I prioritize this for adaptation? Why or why not?

Trend Adaptation Pipeline

Trend Scouting
      Use strategic scrolling and platform research tools to identify potential trends

   3. How long should I run the test before making conclusions?
   4. What would indicate that I should scale this approach?
   5. How might I further iterate if the initial test is promising but not

Implementation Tips
 Use AI as a Starting Point
 These prompts provide excellent direction, but always verify trends through your own
 platform research


 Combine Approaches
 The most effective trends research combines AI analysis with human creativity and
 strategic platform observation


 Stay Platform-Native
 Use these insights to create content that feels natural to each platform rather than trying
 to force a trend


 Test Quickly
 The faster you can test a trend adaptation, the more likely you are to benefit from its momentum


 Document Everything
 Keep a database of trends you've analyzed, tested, and their results to build pattern
 recognition over time




   Remember
  Remember that AI analysis of trends will be based on its training data cutoff.
  Always verify current trends through direct platform research before investing
  significant resources in adaptation.
