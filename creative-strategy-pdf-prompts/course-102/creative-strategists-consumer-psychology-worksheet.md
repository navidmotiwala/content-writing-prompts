   SECTION 7



Creative Evaluation Checklist
Before finalizing your creative, verify:
   Does it clearly address the right awareness level?

   Does it tap into at least one powerful emotional driver?

   Does it emphasize transformation, not just features?

   Does it leverage 2-3 persuasion principles effectively?

   Is the hook strong enough to stop the scroll?

   Is the primary benefit immediately clear?

   Would I be excited to see this ad if I were the target customer?

   Have I created pattern interrupts or distinctive elements?

   Does it address objections the audience might have?

   Is the call-to-action clear and compelling?
