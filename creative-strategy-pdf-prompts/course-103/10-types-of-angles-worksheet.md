   ANGLE TYPE 1



Promotion
   Core Message

   “Because it's on sale”
   The Promotion angle emphasizes limited-time offers, discounts, special deals, or exclusive
   pricing. It creates urgency and appeals to value-conscious customers.




When to Use                                                     Psychological Triggers

   During sales events or seasonal promotions                      Scarcity
   To clear inventory or increase short-term revenue               Loss aversion
   When targeting price-sensitive audiences                        Value perception
   To reactivate dormant customers

   ANGLE TYPE 3



Social Proof
   Core Message

   "Because everyone loves it"
   The Social Proof angle leverages the wisdom of crowds and demonstrates widespread
   adoption. It shows that others have already made the purchase decision and are satisfied.




When to Use                                                      Psychological Triggers

   When you have impressive customer numbers                       Conformity bias
   For products with strong reviews/ratings                        Fear of missing out (FOMO)
   In competitive markets where trust is crucial                   Desire for belonging
   For status-oriented products



Example Applications

    [problem area] with [product]"
    [SERVICE]: "Why 98% of customers renew their subscription after the
    first year"

   ANGLE TYPE 4



Influencer
   Core Message

   "Because this person loves it"
   The Influencer angle uses trusted personalities, experts, or celebrities to build credibility.
   It leverages transferred authority and trust from a specific individual rather than the crowd.




When to Use                                                        Psychological Triggers

   When endorsed by relevant experts or celebrities                  Authority bias
   For products where expertise matters in the decision              Aspirational identity
   When targeting communities that follow influencers                Credibility through association
   To establish authority in new markets



Example Applications

    for [benefit]"

   ANGLE TYPE 9



Comparison
   Core Message

   “Because it's better than our competitors"
   The Comparison angle directly or indirectly contrasts your offering with alternatives.
   It highlights your competitive advantages and unique selling points.




When to Use                                                       Psychological Triggers

   In competitive markets with clear alternatives                    Contrast effect
   When you have specific advantages over competitors                Loss aversion
   When targeting customers with competing options                   Maximization tendency
   For differentiation in saturated markets



Example Applications

   key performance tests"

   [common competitor limitation]"
