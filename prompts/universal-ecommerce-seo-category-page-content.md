Adapt the writing style, headings, examples, and recommendations to the specific product category.

## 3. Content Requirements

### Section A: First Fold Content

**Word Count: 50–80 words**

**Instructions:**

1. Start with an engaging, category-specific introduction.
2. Include the primary keyword naturally within the opening sentences.
3. Briefly explain what customers can expect from the collection.
4. Highlight meaningful product features, styles, benefits, or uses.
5. Address the main customer need or buying motivation.
6. Mention the brand naturally when relevant.
7. Include geographic relevance only when useful.
8. End with a natural invitation to explore the collection.

Avoid exaggerated claims, unnecessary adjectives, generic marketing statements, and keyword stuffing.

## 4. Footer Content

**Total Word Count: 300–400 words, excluding headings.**

Create five relevant sections. Adapt their headings and content to the selected category.

### H2: Buy [Category Name] Online at [Brand Name]

**Suggested Length: 55–70 words**

**Instructions:**

- Introduce the category and available products.
- Highlight the range of styles, features, specifications, or designs.
- Explain the main benefits of exploring this collection.
- Identify the types of customers the category serves.
- Include relevant commercial keywords naturally.
- Explain the convenience of browsing and comparing available options.
- Mention verified brand advantages when relevant.

**Heading Adaptation:**

Do not force the word "Buy" when it sounds unnatural. Use the most relevant commercial heading based on the category and search intent.

### H2: [Category-Specific Features, Benefits or Customization Heading]

**Suggested Length: 55–70 words**

**Instructions:**

Choose the most relevant subject based on the product category.

Possible subjects:

- Personalized or Customizable Products
- Key Features and Specifications
- Materials and Build Quality
- Designs and Styles
- Product Benefits and Practical Uses
- Comfort and Convenience
- Technology and Functionality
- Product Compatibility
- Durability and Maintenance

Explain the selected subject using verified product details.

For customizable products, describe available personalization options and their practical or emotional value.

For non-customizable products, focus on features, specifications, benefits, or other meaningful differentiators.

Avoid creating customization claims for products without personalization options.

### H2: Explore Our Diverse Collection of [Category Name]

**Suggested Length: 55–70 words**

**Instructions:**

- Highlight relevant variations in design, features, functionality, size, or style.
- Explain which products may suit different customer needs.
- Mention important differences between available options.
- Incorporate category-specific secondary keywords.
- Highlight meaningful product variety without repeating earlier sections.
- Use product examples only when confirmed.

## 5. Dynamic Content Adaptation Rules

Before writing, identify the category type and adapt the content accordingly.

| Category Type          | Primary Content Focus                                             |
| ---------------------- | ----------------------------------------------------------------- |
| Wedding Gifts          | Personalization, recipients, occasions, memories                  |
| Home Decor             | Aesthetics, materials, room suitability, design                   |
| Furniture              | Dimensions, materials, comfort, space utilization                 |
| Electronics            | Specifications, performance, compatibility, features              |
| Fashion                | Style, fit, fabric, occasion, comfort                             |
| Beauty                 | Skin or hair suitability, ingredients, product use                |
| Jewellery              | Materials, designs, occasions, care                               |
| Kitchen Products       | Functionality, materials, cleaning, everyday use                  |
| Baby Products          | Age suitability, safety, comfort, materials                       |
| Sports & Fitness       | Activity suitability, fit, performance, durability                |
| Office Supplies        | Productivity, functionality, compatibility                        |
| Personalized Products  | Customization, intended recipient, occasion                       |
| Automotive Accessories | Vehicle compatibility, installation, practical use                |
| Pet Products           | Pet suitability, size, materials, safety                          |
| Other Categories       | Determine relevant selection factors from the product information |

**Rules:**

1. Do not apply gifting-related emotional language to unrelated categories.
2. Do not apply technical specifications to categories where they are irrelevant.
3. Do not assume every product can be personalized.
4. Do not invent subcategories that are absent from the website.
5. Adapt the content to local customer needs where appropriate.
6. Use category-specific purchasing criteria instead of generic recommendations.
7. Adjust headings to match actual product attributes and search intent.
8. Ensure the content explains why the category is useful and how customers can choose suitable products.

## 6. SEO Optimization Guidelines

1. Include the primary keyword naturally in the first fold.
2. Use the primary keyword in a relevant H2 where appropriate.
3. Distribute secondary keywords based on context.
4. Include long-tail variations only when they fit naturally.
5. Use related terminology that accurately describes the products.
6. Align the content with commercial or transactional search intent.
7. Address important informational questions without shifting away from the category's purpose.
8. Avoid keyword stuffing and unnatural exact-match repetition.
9. Use descriptive, relevant internal anchor text.
10. Avoid linking to unrelated categories.
11. Ensure the content adds information beyond the visible product listings.
12. Avoid copying product descriptions or competitor content.
13. Differentiate content across similar category pages.
14. Do not create unnecessary keyword variations solely to increase word count.
15. Keep headings useful and descriptive rather than keyword-heavy.

## 7. Writing Guidelines

- Use clear, natural, conversational English.
- Adapt spelling and terminology to the target country.
- Maintain a professional, helpful, customer-focused tone.
- Prioritize useful product information over promotional language.
- Use short sentences and readable paragraphs.
- Avoid repetitive sentence structures.
- Avoid unnecessary adjectives and exaggerated claims.
- Do not use em dashes or emojis.
- Avoid generic AI phrases such as "delve into," "look no further," "treasure trove," "elevate your experience," "unlock the potential," "seamless blend," and "perfect solution."
- Avoid robotic transitions.
- Do not repeat identical benefits across sections.
- Avoid overusing the brand name.
- Do not add artificial emotional language.
- Do not make claims about quality, pricing, shipping, availability, or guarantees without evidence.
- Avoid filler content written only to meet the word count.
- Maintain accurate grammar, spelling, and punctuation.

## 8. Research and Accuracy Requirements

Before generating content:

1. Review the supplied category URL and website when web access is available.
2. Identify actual products, subcategories, and visible product attributes.
3. Check whether mentioned product features are supported by the supplied information.
4. Identify the most relevant customer needs and purchase considerations.
5. Use supplied keyword research rather than inventing search volumes, rankings, or keyword difficulty.
6. Check whether the page already answers important customer questions.
7. Identify opportunities to explain features, compare product types, or address purchase uncertainty.
8. Verify internal URLs before linking.
9. Distinguish verified information from assumptions.
10. If browsing is unavailable, work from the provided details and omit unsupported specifics.
