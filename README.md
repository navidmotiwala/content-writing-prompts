# Content Writing Prompts

A practical collection of reusable content writing prompts for SEO, Answer Engine Optimization (AEO), Generative Engine Optimization (GEO), and AI search.

The prompts focus on accurate information, natural writing, complete search intent coverage, and clear editorial requirements. They do not promise search rankings or inclusion in AI-generated answers.

## Prompt Library

| Prompt | Description |
| --- | --- |
| [Universal SEO, AEO, GEO and AI Search Content Writing Master Prompt](prompts/universal-seo-aeo-geo-ai-search-content-writing.md) | Complete article-writing workflow covering research, keyword mapping, source verification, semantic structure, internal links, metadata, and final quality checks. |

## How to Use

1. Open the prompt from the library above.
2. Copy the complete prompt into the AI assistant of choice.
3. Replace the bracketed input placeholders with the article details.
4. Review the draft, verify sources, check brand requirements, and make any necessary editorial changes before publishing.

## Included in the Master Prompt

- Content brief, audience, topic, keywords, and search intent
- Research and factual verification requirements
- Primary, secondary, long-tail, and semantic keyword suggestions
- H1, H2, and H3 article hierarchy
- Natural writing rules and a list of overused phrases to avoid
- Passage-level readability for AEO, GEO, and AI retrieval
- Relevant entities, related questions, and topic completeness
- Anchor text and target URL placement
- Meta title and description limits
- Section-level keyword and word-count mapping
- Publication checklist and quality assurance

## Notes

This is a reusable editorial template, not a guarantee of SEO performance. Keyword volumes, ranking factors, statistics, and other claims should be verified using appropriate tools and sources.
